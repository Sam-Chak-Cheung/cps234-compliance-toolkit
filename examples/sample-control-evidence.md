# Sample CPS 234 Control Evidence Record

## CPS 234 Area

Testing control effectiveness

## Control

Privileged access review

## Evidence

- Current privileged role export
- Access review approval record
- Exception register
- Remediation ticket list
- Date of last review

## Assessment Notes

The control is stronger when access review evidence is linked to named control owners, dated approvals, exception decisions, and completed remediation actions.

## Open Questions

- Are all privileged access paths included, including cloud, network, endpoint, and SaaS platforms?
- Are third-party administrator accounts included?
- Is the review frequency aligned to the criticality of the asset?
